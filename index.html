How It Works

One simple interface for digital execution.

STEP 01

Describe

Tell Inklur what outcome you want using normal language. No complicated workflows or technical commands.

STEP 02

Understand & Plan

Inklur interprets your request and creates a sequence of actions required to accomplish the task.

STEP 03

Execute

Connected services and APIs can perform the required actions. Sensitive actions can require your approval.

The Vision

From AI that answers to AI that executes.

One interface. Many digital services.

The long-term vision of Inklur is to make digital work as simple as describing what you want. Instead of manually switching between applications, users could give one instruction and let an AI execution layer coordinate the required services.
